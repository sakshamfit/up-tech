## Identity

The header uses government artwork identifying the organisations named on the site. Text reading “TGTS” is a typographic label, not a claim to reproduce a separate official agency logo. Government marks identify the organisations; they do not imply endorsement.

Leadership names and roles are corroborated by the State Portal council of ministers and the IT, E&C directory:

- https://www.telangana.gov.in/government/council-of-ministers/
- https://www.telangana.gov.in/departments/information-technology-electronics-and-communications/

`dist/assets/official/provenance.json` records the exact portrait and government artwork sources. The CM and minister portraits and combined Telangana emblem / Telangana Rising artwork come from the official State Portal. The chairman portrait is from the Telangana Meeseva Operators Association, which captions the individual as TGTSL Chairman Sri Manne Satish Kumar; the government directory corroborates the role and spelling used in this site.

Client artwork sources:

- IGRS government emblem: https://notary.igrs.telangana.gov.in/resources/img/telanganalogo.png (government portal).
- Telangana Rising: https://notary.igrs.telangana.gov.in/resources/img/telangana-rising.png (government portal).
- TGTRANSCO: https://portal.tgtransco.com/static/images/logo_left.png (institutional portal).
- GHMC: https://workmodule.ghmc.bloomsolutions.co.in/assets/cms/images/ghmc.png (GHMC works monitoring portal).
- Telangana Police: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/0/01/Telangana_Police_Logo.png (public reproduction).
- Hyderabad Police: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/5/5d/HyderabadPoliceLogo.jpg (public reproduction).
- TS GENCO: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/c/cb/TSGENCOlogo.png (public reproduction of the historic mark).

The two police marks and GENCO mark are separately sourced public reproductions. The use of the current TGTRANSCO mark is intentional; the site also refers to its former TS TRANSCO name.
